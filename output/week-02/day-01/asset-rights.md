No creator image, voice recording, portrait or stock asset is used.

Production uses free/open-source Java/Spring, Python, Pillow, NumPy, SciPy, local Kokoro/Whisper alignment and FFmpeg/FFprobe tools. Dependency versions, model/style hashes, font provenance and reproduction code are retained. Downloaded speech models, virtual environments, caches and bytecode are excluded from delivery.

Narration is synthetic, from licensed pretrained styles; it is not a human recording or a named person's imitation. Perceived young-to-middle adult male age, smoothness and final pronunciation still require actual listening acceptance. No auditory approval is inferred from model labels, ASR, G2P, pitch or waveform metrics. Current platform disclosure and monetization decisions require the manual review recorded in platform-policy-review.md. No automatic upload or publication is performed.
