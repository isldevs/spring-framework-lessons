Who creates your Java objects? ☕

With manual construction, your code creates a repository and passes it to a service. With Spring IoC, the container creates and wires the managed beans you register. Your application can then request the ready service with getBean().

That shift in control is inversion of control. Spring manages registered beans; other Java objects can still be created by your code.
